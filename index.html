Clear Answers

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about how we work and what to expect.

Do you make the ad creatives? +

No. SIMPLE only RUNS ads. We do not design ad creatives. You provide the creative (image photo, product banner, or video reel), and SIMPLE handles the entire campaign setup, audience targeting, budget allocation, and continuous running and optimization of your campaigns.

Which platforms do you run ads on? +

We run ads on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, X, and other major social platforms. Google Ads is also available as an optional service. We will review your business type and recommend the exact platforms that bring the best return for your products.

How long does setup take? +

Setup typically takes between 3 to 7 business days once we receive your business details, catalog info, and creative assets. We provide a transparent day-by-day timeline before we start.

Can the AI agent be on both WhatsApp and Instagram? +

Yes, absolutely! You can choose the option for single platform (WhatsApp or Instagram) or both platforms together. When deployed on both, the AI assistant maintains consistent, accurate business information across both channels.

What does the “free ad run” include? +

Our included free ad run covers complete audience research, account setup, ad group creation, targeting parameters, and management of the campaign. The ad spend budget is discussed and agreed with you in advance, and you provide the image or video creative.

Do I need any technical knowledge? +

Not at all. That is the whole point of SIMPLE! You simply tell us about your brand, products, and prices in everyday language. We take care of the domain, hosting, code, API connections, and ad manager configurations.
