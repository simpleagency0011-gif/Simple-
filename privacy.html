Privacy Policy.

Last Updated: October 2026 · Effective Date: October 2026 · SIMPLE Agency

01. Introduction

At SIMPLE Agency ("SIMPLE," "we," "our," or "us"), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share with us. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of information obtained through our website (https://simple-chi-nine.vercel.app/) and associated service channels.

By accessing our site, submitting an inquiry, or interacting with our automated services, you acknowledge the terms outlined in this policy.

02. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our digital services, respond to project inquiries, and analyze aggregate site usage:

  • • Direct Inquiry Information: When you submit our contact form, we collect your full name, contact phone number or email address, selected service interest, platform preferences, and any optional project notes you provide.
  • • Automated Telemetry & Analytics Data: When you browse our website, we collect non-identifiable technical data including your browser type, device type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and general geographic location (country/city level).
  • • Assistant Interaction Data: Queries submitted through our floating on-page assistant are processed strictly to generate responses and are not sold to third parties.

03. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • • To respond to your project inquiries and provide tailored quotes within 24 business hours.
  • • To configure, deliver, and support your custom websites, AI agents, and advertising campaigns.
  • • To monitor website performance, prevent malicious bot submissions, and ensure HTTPS security.
  • • To analyze aggregate usage patterns using Google Analytics in order to improve site usability.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party data brokers or marketing lists.

04. Cookies & Google Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar storage technologies:

• Google Analytics (GA4): We use Google Analytics with IP anonymization enabled (anonymize_ip: true) to track aggregated visitor telemetry. Google Analytics sets cookies (_ga, _ga_*) to distinguish unique visitors without storing personally identifiable data. You can opt out of Google Analytics across the web using Google's opt-out browser add-on.

• Cookie Consent Preference: We store your cookie banner preference in your browser's local storage (simple_cookie_consent) so we remember whether you accepted analytics cookies.

05. Third-Party Services

In delivering our digital infrastructure, we interact with reputable third-party infrastructure providers that maintain strict industry security standards:

  • • Hosting: Vercel Inc. and GitHub Pages (secure global edge distribution networks).
  • • Analytics: Google LLC (Google Analytics 4).
  • • Ad & Messaging Platforms: Meta Platforms Inc. (WhatsApp Cloud API, Instagram DM, Facebook Ads) and Google Ads when client campaigns are established.

06. Data Security & Your Rights

All data in transit is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS). We maintain strict access boundaries and do not store sensitive client passwords or credit card numbers on our static web servers.

You retain the following rights regarding your data:

  • • The right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • • The right to request the correction or permanent deletion of your inquiry data.
  • • The right to withdraw consent for analytics tracking at any time.
